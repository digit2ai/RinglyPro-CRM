Plain terms

What we are, and what we are not

We are

  • A technology platformWe gather active new-construction communities and builder incentives, and lay them out side by side in monthly-payment terms.
  • Verified, with a dateWe only show incentives a licensed agent verified, each with the date it was last checked. Fees we can't confirm say "not yet confirmed", never $0.
  • Free for buyersYou pay nothing to use BuyersLine or to be connected with the agent who represents you.
  • Paid by the agentThe licensed agent pays BuyersLine a fee when you meet with them. That is how the platform is paid.

We are not

  • Not a real estate brokerageRepresentation comes from a licensed Florida real estate sales associate and their brokerage, not from BuyersLine.
  • Not a lenderPayment figures are estimates. They are not loan offers, pre-qualifications or approvals.
  • Not a builder's sales officeWe don't sell homes for any builder. Your agent represents you, the buyer.
