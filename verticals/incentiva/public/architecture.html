BuyersLine is a free platform that helps people buy new-construction homes in Tampa Bay. It is a technology platform, not a brokerage and not a lender. This page shows what runs underneath it.

01 · The model

One brain, seven agents

Buyers talk only to Ana. Ana reads the live state of the page and drives the other agents. A licensed agent verifies promotions and meets the buyer. Every price, payment and score is computed by deterministic rules, never by a model.

ONE VOICE

Ana is the single point of contact by chat or voice, in English and Spanish. The other agents work behind her.

LIVE PAGE

She sees what the buyer sees: answers so far, what is missing, the communities on screen and the calculator result.

HUMAN

A licensed Florida sales associate verifies every promotion before it can reach a report, and meets the buyer.

RULES

Models write words. Buying power, payments, fit ranking and readiness come from fixed engines.

02 · Agent roster

Who does what

Seven AI agents. Each one has a trigger, a model or rule set, and guardrails written in code.

01 · Brain

Ana, the guide

Live

The buyer's only conversational contact, by voice or chat, in English and Spanish. Knows the whole process, never repeats a question, answers, then returns to the next step.

Model
Claude Haiku
Trigger
The buyer talks or types
Inputs
Live page state: answers, missing items, communities on screen, calculator result
Outputs
Record answers · choose communities the buyer named · run the buying power calculator · scroll to a section · create the report after a clear yes
Guardrails
  • Cannot tick contact consent boxes
  • Creates the report only after a clear yes plus a 5-second cancel
  • Offline mode speaks the question on screen
02 · Agent

Promotions Researcher

Live

Searches builder websites for current promotions, rates and closing-cost offers near the buyer's area.

Model
Claude Sonnet with web search
Trigger
Stage 3 of the conversation. Results cached 24 hours per area and shared.
Inputs
The buyer's area, looked up on the server from ZIP and place
Outputs
Promotion rows per community for the buyer to choose from
Guardrails
  • "Source found" only when the search actually returned the source
  • Expired, placeholder and fair-housing-flagged rows hidden
  • No links shown to buyers
  • Monthly cap
03 · Agent

Incentive Reader

Live

Reads builder web pages and pasted broker emails and extracts each incentive.

Model
Claude Sonnet, keyword fallback
Trigger
A builder page is checked, or a broker email is pasted into the console
Inputs
Builder web pages · pasted broker emails
Outputs

  • Any amount, rate or date not in the source becomes a question for the licensed agent

05 · Agent

Rachel, follow-up

Live for emailSMS needs sender setup

Keeps in touch for 90 days in the buyer's language, and alerts when a promotion in a community they chose changes or disappears.

Runs on
Fixed bilingual messages on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 (texts on 1, 7, 30, 90) · Claude Haiku may reword one opening sentence, discarded if it adds a figure or a name
Trigger
A lead is saved · a promotion in a chosen community changes or disappears
Inputs
The buyer's language, contact choices and chosen communities
Channels
Email · SMS when the buyer consented and a sender number is configured · WhatsApp not built yet
Guardrails
  • Only channels the buyer ticked, re-checked at send time
  • Double opt-in: texts only after the buyer replies YES, follow-up emails only after they open their report link
  • Quiet hours 8 pm to 9 am Eastern for SMS
  • Promotion alerts compare two AI research runs and say the offer is unconfirmed until the agent checks it
  • One-click unsubscribe
  • STOP honored
06 · Agent

Hand-off agent

Live (email)SMS brief needs sender setup

Scores readiness with fixed rules into Hot, Warm or Nurture, sends the licensed agent a buyer brief within a minute, and alerts the owner when a new lead has no agent response after 4 hours.

Runs on
Fixed scoring rules (the score is never a model) · Claude Haiku may reword the suggested opening line, discarded if it adds a figure or a name
Trigger
Every saved lead is scored · the agent is alerted only when the buyer agreed to agent contact
Inputs
Timing · financing · communities chosen · agent status · visits · phone
Outputs
Hot, Warm or Nurture with reasons · buyer brief and suggested opening to the licensed agent · alert to the owner and the agent after 4 hours with no response
Guardrails
  • Only leads that agreed to agent contact
  • The brief carries no contact details outside the console
07 · Agent

Scheduler

Live

Lets the buyer book a 30-minute consult inside the agent's weekly hours, sends reminders, then asks buyer and agent how the meeting went.

Runs on
Rules only, no model: the licensed agent's weekly hours (Monday to Friday, 9 to 5 until set in the console) and existing bookings
Trigger
The buyer books a consult
Inputs
Open 30-minute slots in Eastern time
Outputs
A booked consult · reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before · a how-did-it-go question to buyer and agent
Guardrails
  • Only leads that agreed to agent contact
  • Times in Eastern time, at least 2 hours ahead
  • No double booking, one active consult per buyer
  • Reminder texts only with text consent

05 · Enforcement

Guardrails that are code, not promises

These hold because the software refuses the alternative, not because a prompt asks nicely.

RULE 01

Only verified, fresh, unexpired incentives in reports

An offer that is withdrawn, stale or past its date drops out of the report.

RULE 02

Models write words, never figures

Payments, buying power and scores come from the deterministic engines.

RULE 03

Consent recorded in the server's own wording

Stored with a timestamp, per channel, in the buyer's language.

RULE 04

A buyer under contract with another agent is not contacted

No hand-off, no follow-up, no brief.

RULE 05

Contact boxes are always the buyer's own tap

Ana can fill answers. She cannot tick a consent box.

RULE 06

Nothing sends without consent

Email and SMS go only on the channels the buyer chose.

RULE 07

Compliance check blocks fair-housing language

A report that trips it is held, not released.

RULE 08

Only a licensed account can verify an incentive

The platform owner's account cannot confirm one.
