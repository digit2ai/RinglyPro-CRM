The rules

Trust is built into the code.

NUMBERS

The AI never writes a figure

Prices, payments and buying power are calculated. A sentence that adds a number is discarded.

PROMOTIONS

Compliance before display

Guarantees, expired offers, fair-housing wording and offers with no source are held back.

CONSENT

The buyer ticks the box

No voice, form or AI can share a buyer's details. Without consent, nobody contacts them.

HONESTY

Says "none" when there is none

Best deal stays empty rather than crowning an offer that is not real.

ACCESS

Maker and checker

Every login is approved by the owner. Agents see only their own leads.

THE BUSINESS

Technology, not a brokerage

Free for buyers. No fee tied to an agreement, a registration or a closing.
