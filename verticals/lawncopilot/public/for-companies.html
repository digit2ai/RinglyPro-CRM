What each one takes off your plate

The Receptionist

Every call answered, including the ones at 9pm on a Sunday that currently go to voicemail and then to your competitor. Captures the name, number, email, and address, answers the usual questions, books the job, and hands off to you when a caller needs a person.

The Estimator

Pulls the parcel record, measures the lawn, subtracts the driveway and patio, applies your rate card, and returns a line-itemized price. No windshield time. Anything it is unsure of goes to your review queue instead of out the door at the wrong price.

The Dispatcher

Owns the calendar and real crew capacity, so nothing gets double-booked and no date is promised that you cannot serve. Handles reschedules, skips, and pauses. Moves a whole day and notifies every customer when weather hits.

The Administrator

Invoices the minute a visit is marked complete. Runs autopay with advance notice. Works the failed-card retry ladder so you are not the one sending awkward texts. Keeps the AR aging and exports the books tax-ready.
