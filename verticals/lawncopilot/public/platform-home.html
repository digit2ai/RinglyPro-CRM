Questions

Do I need a website already?

No, and most landscapers do not have one. Your Lawn Co-Pilot page is your website — you point your Google listing at it and put the QR code on your truck. There is no domain to buy and nothing to build.

How can it quote without seeing the yard?

It measures the lot from public property records, subtracts the house footprint, driveway and walkways, and prices what is left against your rate card. When a record cannot be verified it says so and flags it for you before anything is charged.

Do I lose control of my pricing?

No. The rate card is yours, set by county, city, ZIP, property type and frequency. The Controller can recommend a change with the numbers behind it, but nothing changes a price except you.

Is it really answering my phone?

Yes, on your own number, as your company. It tells callers it is an AI if they ask, and transfers to you whenever someone wants a person.

What about payroll taxes?

Lawn Co-Pilot computes payroll from real clocked hours and presents it for your approval. Filing and remittance go through a licensed payroll provider — we never claim to have filed anything we did not.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Your data is yours and exports on request.

What if I already use another tool?

Run alongside for a season. The difference is that other tools organize the work and hand it back to you. This one does the work and hands you the approvals.
