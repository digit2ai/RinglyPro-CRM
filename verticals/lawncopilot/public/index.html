Questions

Is the price real, or a rough range?

It is a real price built from your measured lawn area and a published rate card. When we could not get a verified parcel record, we say so on the quote and mark it preliminary — and a person checks it before you are ever charged.

How do you measure my lawn without coming out?

Parcel records give the lot size and boundary, building footprint data gives the house, and we subtract driveway, walkways, and patio. What is left is the lawn. Every figure shows its source, and anything we inferred instead of measured is labeled.

What if the measurement is wrong?

Tap the correction request on your quote or in your portal. It goes to a human, who can adjust the serviceable area. The change is logged and your price updates from the corrected number.

Am I talking to a real person?

No. The receptionist is an AI, and it will tell you that the moment you ask. You can ask for a human at any point and it will transfer you or take a message.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Recurring plans get a better per-visit rate, but you can skip, pause, or cancel from your portal, subject to the notice period on your plan.

What happens when it rains?

The visit moves to the next available day and you are notified. You are not charged for a visit that did not happen.

Is my card safe?

We never store card numbers. Payments run through a compliant third-party processor; our records hold only the card brand, last four digits, and expiration for display.

What areas do you serve?

Florida to start. Enter your address and the system confirms coverage before quoting.
