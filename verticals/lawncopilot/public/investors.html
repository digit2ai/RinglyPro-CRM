The problem

They can't afford an office. So they are the office — at 9pm.

Ninety percent of landscaping companies are one to three trucks. They do a full day of labor, then a second full-time job no one pays them for.

The call goes unansweredThe phone rings while the mower is running. The lead calls the next company on the list.

The invoice that never goes outWork done, never billed. Cash sits in a truck's glovebox as scribbled notes.

Why now

Two things just became true at once.

The window opens when the cost curve and the incumbents' blind spot line up. They just did.

Go to market

We acquire like Vagaro, not like a website builder.
