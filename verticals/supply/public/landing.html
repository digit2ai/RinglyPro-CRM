Your inventory, called into the right contractors' phones.

RinglyPro Supply is AI outbound sales for hardware stores and building-material suppliers. It reads your catalog, works out which contractors buy each product, compares your price only against prices you have verified, and has an AI agent call the right contractors. When they call back, it already knows them. When they buy, it knows which campaign earned the sale.

Eight questions it answers

  1. What do we have to sell?
  2. What is it worth in the current market? Only from competitor prices you checked, with the date.
  3. Who is most likely to buy it?
  4. What should we offer them?
  5. Who should we call first?
  6. What happened during the call?
  7. What do we know when they call back?
  8. Which campaign generated the sale?

Nothing dials by itself

A campaign is a draft until an owner approves and activates it. Do Not Call and opt-outs are checked when a contractor is queued and again at dial time.

No invented numbers

The agent quotes your prices and a saving only when a same-unit competitor price was verified within your freshness window. Otherwise it makes no comparison.

Runs on GoHighLevel

Calls, AI voice agents, contacts and pipelines live in your GoHighLevel sub-account. The sales intelligence lives here, behind a provider layer, so the carrier can change without losing it.
