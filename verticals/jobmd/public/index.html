The Market

Surgeon recruitment has never been more daunting — on both sides of the market.

The demand for skilled, talented surgeons is higher now than ever before. A report released by the American Association of Medical Colleges projects shortages of 15,000 – 30,000 in all surgical specialties by 2034. The high cost of medical education is contributing to this ongoing physician shortage. In today's medical recruitment market, there are increasing challenges in identifying, attracting, placing, and retaining top medical talent.

It isn't just about filling a position. It is about finding the most skilled and talented surgeon available — the surgeons who deliver the best clinical outcomes for their patients and the hospitals they represent, who can consolidate markets and change referral patterns in the markets they move to and work within.

Patients are more educated now than ever. Patients research the surgeons in their area and their track record of success to ensure they will receive the best care available to them. Patients can easily locate clinical outcome data online to ensure they have the best chance at an excellent clinical outcome based on the surgeon they select for their procedure.

Our interests are aligned with the surgeons and hospitals we work with. Surgeons want to work for the top ranked hospitals, and hospitals want to recruit the top ranked surgeons who will deliver the best care.
