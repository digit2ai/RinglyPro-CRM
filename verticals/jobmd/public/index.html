The Market

Healthcare recruitment has never been more daunting — on both sides of the market.

The demand for skilled, talented clinicians is higher now than ever before. A report released by the American Association of Medical Colleges projects shortages of 15,000 – 30,000 in all surgical specialties by 2034, and the same pressure runs through physician and medical staff hiring right across the hospital. The high cost of medical education is contributing to this ongoing shortage. In today's medical recruitment market, there are increasing challenges in identifying, attracting, placing, and retaining top medical talent.

It isn't just about filling a position. It is about finding the most skilled and talented person available — the surgeons and physicians who deliver the best clinical outcomes for their patients and the hospitals they represent, who can consolidate markets and change referral patterns in the markets they move to and work within, and the medical staff who make those outcomes repeatable.

Patients are more educated now than ever. Patients research the surgeons and physicians in their area and their track record of success to ensure they will receive the best care available to them. Patients can easily locate clinical outcome data online to ensure they have the best chance at an excellent clinical outcome based on who they select for their care.

Our interests are aligned with the clinicians and hospitals we work with. Clinicians want to work for the top ranked hospitals, and hospitals want to recruit the top ranked people who will deliver the best care.
