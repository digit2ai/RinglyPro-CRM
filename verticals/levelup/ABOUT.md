# LevelUp Media Marketing

**Same you. Bigger things.**

## Who we are

LevelUp Media Marketing is the AI back office for content creators. We give every creator a team of AI agents that plans the content, writes the scripts, prepares the edits and handles the business side, so the creator can spend their time doing what only they can do: creating.

We are built for creators at every stage. You might be just starting and still looking for your niche, or you might already work with brands and sell products every week. Either way, we meet you where you are and hold your hand from there.

Three beliefs guide everything we build:

- **Helpful and friendly first.** Every screen should make your day lighter, never heavier.
- **Your voice, not ours.** Everything we produce is shaped by you: your brand, your goals, your style.
- **You stay in control.** Nothing is posted, sent or agreed without your OK.

## What we do

LevelUp works like a small, dedicated team that never sleeps. You talk to one manager, **Líder**, and Líder hands the work to the right specialist, then reports back to you.

## Your team learns you

This is what makes LevelUp different. You can **train every agent**:

- Add knowledge: your brand story, your audience, your do's and don'ts.
- Add rules: short corrections like "never use the word hack" or "always start on the first word of the hook".
- Choose who learns it: the whole team, or just one agent.
- Test it: ask an agent the same question with and without your training and see the difference.

And when the same mistake happens three times in your edits, LevelUp notices and offers to turn it into a rule, so you correct it once instead of every video.

## How we keep you safe

Two promises hold everywhere in LevelUp:

1. **Read only stays read only.** Anything that touches your inbox or accounts only reads and drafts. It never sends a reply, closes a deal or acts for you without your approval.
2. **Posts start as drafts.** Every video is a draft you review and publish yourself. When you trust the quality, you can choose scheduled posting for a specific account, pause it any time, and see a full log.

On top of that:

- Brand replies only ever quote prices from **your own rate card**.
- Scripts copy a proven structure, **never someone else's words**.
- Suspicious brand emails are flagged in plain words, and anything that looks like a scam is marked before you reply.
- We only connect to platforms through their official programs. We never use tricks that could put your accounts at risk.
- Your data, rates and training belong to your account alone.
