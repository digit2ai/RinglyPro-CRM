The Paradox of 2026

Everyone adopted AI. Almost no one is winning with it.

  • 88% of organizations now use AI in at least one business function (McKinsey)
  • Only ~7% have scaled AI across the enterprise
  • Only ~6% capture significant, company-wide value from it
  • 80–95% of enterprise AI projects fail to deliver their promised ROI (RAND / MIT)

“The problem is not the AI. It is what the AI is plugged into.”

The Real Problem · The Traditional Operating Model

Roughly 9 out of 10 enterprises still run on a pre-AI operating model.

They bolt AI tools onto:

  • Siloed departments and manual handoffs
  • Legacy systems that don’t talk to each other
  • Headcount-based cost structures
  • Decisions that wait for meetings

AI on top of a traditional operating model produces expensive pilots — not transformation. 60% of AI projects without AI-ready architecture are expected to be abandoned through 2026 (Gartner).

  • Organizations that redesign work around AI are 2x more likely to exceed revenue goals (Gartner)

“The gap between AI-native operators and traditional operators widens every quarter — and it will not close later at the same price.”
