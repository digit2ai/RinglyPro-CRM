The price is high for three reasons. Two are real costs. The third is that the original estimate was too cautious.

2. Reason 1 — the client pays double the cost

The quote multiplies the cost by 1.70 for margin and then adds 19% IVA on top. Every dollar Digit2AI spends becomes $2.02 on Planea's invoice. Most of the "this is too expensive" feeling comes from here, not from the hours.

4. Reason 3 — the hours were estimated as a hand-built job

This is the part that was wrong. The estimate treated each document type as a custom parser. With a model that reads PDFs and screenshots natively, a document reader is mostly a schema plus testing against real samples. The estimate also carried hardening Planea never asked for, such as rewriting the upload path and a very broad test suite.

The warning sign. The entire Fase 0 PoC, a matching engine plus a financial simulation, was estimated at 160 hours. Option 2 priced reading three documents at 154 hours. That is the size of a whole product, not one feature.

The Calendario is the most inflated

Option 1 is 104 hours for a list of dates and reminders. Two lines explain most of it:

  • 14 hours are WhatsApp, which cannot launch until Meta approves the message templates. That work waits on a third party either way.
  • 14 hours derive dates from uploaded documents, and those documents only exist if Planea also buys Option 2 or 3. Sold alone, the calendar has nothing to read.

6. The lean version

Still at $70/hour, still with 70% margin and IVA. Only the hours changed.

OptionHoursCostQuoteQuote beforeDelivery
1. Calendario Planea48$3,360$6,797$14,7272 weeks
2. Fase 1 (3 documents)93$6,510$13,170$21,8083 weeks
3. Alcance completo (11 documents)158$11,060$22,374$39,0844 weeks

What was cut

  • Per-document hours, roughly in half
  • Rewriting the upload path (the existing tax-doc upload is reused)
  • Breadth of the test suite
  • WhatsApp and SMS, moved to a later phase

What was NOT cut

  • The verifier that stops the AI from inventing numbers (Planea's rule 5.7)
  • The HEIC decoder for iPhone photos
  • Handling several bank products in one screenshot
  • The tests that prove no invented figure reaches a user
The risk of the lean version. It only holds if Planea sends the real bank-statement and payroll samples before the build starts. Those are the two highest-volume documents and they are still on Planea's pending list. If samples arrive late or the documents are messier than expected, the hours move back toward the original estimate.
