Key Callout

The diagnostic surface narrowed. Q1's three DB-side patterns — JDBC Failure, HikariPool Saturation and NPE / App Bug — now resolve as a single owned category, taking five investigation tracks down to three, a 40% reduction. No new failure mode emerged at any point in Q2.
Occurrence volume rose to 69 as coverage deepened. Every one was triaged to root cause, and 90% now carry a permanent, source-side or scheduled fix — including PRIN-4619, released mid-quarter, which stopped the DSMT-ISG NULL violations outright.
Fewer distinct problems to solve, each with a known owner — the basis for a materially lower Q3.

MCA (15)GAU taxonomy and delegate attributes App Bug (15): Two distinct source-side changes, neither communicated in advance.
· Timestamp format — the source began sending timestamps in a different layout to the one previously supplied, which the parser could not read. Failures clustered in May and June.
· Field length — "GAU_OWNER_DELEGATES" arrived at 4,001 characters against a 4,000-character column definition.		 PRIN-5573 introduced a common timestamp layer that resolves multiple formats dynamically, so a future source-side format change no longer breaks the parse. The oversized attribute is trimmed to 4,000 characters on load under an INC; the field is not consumed downstream, so no schema change was warranted. PRIN-5573

Standard Operating Procedure – Dataverse SLA Monitoring

End-to-end workflow from daily monitoring through MCA tester attestation

DAILY MONITORING
Step 1 – MonitorIP Data Ingestion Team monitors dashboard daily for SLA status
Step 2 – AlertSLA breach triggers automatic notification to L2 Support
Step 3 – TicketL2 raises Incident Ticket in ServiceNow assigned to CTI NA EX US CTI GL EX CITI DATAVERSE L3
Step 4 – ResolveL3 investigates and resolves root cause
Step 5 – ReloadJob is re-triggered to reload data
QUARTERLY REVIEW
Step 1 – ReviewControl Owner reviews all SLA breach reports quarterly
Step 2 – Permanent FixFor repeated issues, development team applies permanent fixes
Step 3 – ExportControl Owner exports report and reviews MissedCutoff items
Step 4 – EvidenceReport uploaded as MCA evidence for the quarter
Step 5 – ArchiveDocumentation archived in control repository
MCA TESTER REVIEW
Step 1 – SubmitControl Owner submits attested quarterly snapshot to MCA Tester
Step 2 – ValidateTester validates evidence against control requirements
Step 3 – AttestMCA Tester marks attestation complete upon successful validation
Step 4 – RecordFormal sign-off recorded in MCA system
