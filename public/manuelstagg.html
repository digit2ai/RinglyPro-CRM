Professional Profile

Manuel Stagg is a Senior SME and Full-Stack AI Solutions Architect with more than 24 years in the Banking Industry, where he led global compliance, risk, KYC/AML, sanctions-screening and enterprise technology programs at institutional scale. He now designs and ships MCP Neural Intelligence — an AI reasoning layer that connects a business's real systems (CRM, telephony, financials, inventory, compliance and vendor data) through the open Model Context Protocol and reasons across all of them to surface what matters and prescribe the next action.

His differentiator is rare: the same person who understands a Tier-1 bank's control environment also architects, codes, tests and deploys the AI that automates it. He works end-to-end — requirements, data model, backend, voice/agent layer, front end, and production release — across banking, freight, healthcare, retail, education and B2B networks, in English and Spanish.
