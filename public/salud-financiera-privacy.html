Privacy Policy

Salud Financiera, a product of Digit2Ai LLC. Effective October 26, 2026.

Who we are

Salud Financiera is a private personal finance dashboard operated by Digit2Ai LLC. Contact: mstagg@digit2ai.com.

What we collect

  • Account sign-in details (email and a hashed password).
  • With your permission, read-only data from your bank through Plaid: account name and last four digits, transactions, and recurring charges.
  • Budget entries and notes you type into the dashboard.

We never receive or store your bank username or password. You enter them only on your bank's own page through Plaid.

How we use it

  • To show your balances, compare real spending with your plan, and project upcoming payments.
  • To find recurring charges and alert you to changes.

We do not move money, sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Plaid

We use Plaid Inc. to connect to your bank. By connecting, you agree that Plaid may collect and process your information as described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Service providers

  • Plaid (bank connection), Render (hosting and database), and Anthropic (the optional in-app assistant, which may read transaction descriptions to answer your questions; your bank access token is never shared).

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Data is stored in an encrypted database, and the bank access token has an extra layer of encryption. Only the account owner can see the financial data.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data only while you use the dashboard.
  • You can disconnect your bank at any time from the dashboard; this revokes Plaid's access and deletes the stored access token.
  • To delete all your data, email mstagg@digit2ai.com. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
