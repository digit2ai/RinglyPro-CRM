The Fellowship

From Latin American talent to global leadership.

The Visionarium Fellowship is the flagship of the Vision2Ai ecosystem: a nine-month, fully sponsor-funded program launching from Miami in Fall 2026. Every participant — Fellow or open-community member — gets Lina, a bilingual AI mentor with voice, memory, and 24/7 availability, built on the same production platform as the AI Factory.

  • Open TierFree to any young person aged 16–22 across the Americas — no cap, no cost, no application.
  • Fellows40 selected per cohort. Nine months, human mentors, Miami immersions, and a shipped capstone at Demo Day.
  • BilingualEnglish and Spanish as equal operating languages — by design, not by translation.
  • OutcomesPlacements, startups, and scholarships measured publicly — not hours delivered.
Every fellow has something most Ivy League undergraduates do not: a dedicated, bilingual, always-available mentor who knows their goals, their pace, and their name. Cada becario tiene un mentor bilingüe, dedicado y siempre disponible que conoce sus metas, su ritmo y su nombre.
