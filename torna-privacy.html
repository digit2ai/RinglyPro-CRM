Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026 · Last updated 18 August 2026 · Version 1.0

Torna asks you to describe your business problem and then builds software from that description. What you type is often commercially sensitive, and it is processed by AI. This policy says exactly what happens to it.

The short version. We collect your account details, the description of what you want built, any document you attach, and what the AI produces for you. Attached files are read in memory and never written to disk — only the extracted text is used. Your description and that text are sent to Anthropic to do the work, and are not used to train models. Your workspace is private to your account. We do not sell your data. Torna is free while in preview, so there is no payment processor and no card data anywhere. Email info@digit2ai.com to export or delete everything.

4. AI processing

Torna is an AI product; there is no version of it that does not send your description to a model. To be concrete about what that means:

  • Your description, the structured details and the text extracted from your attachments are sent to Anthropic's API (Claude models) to scope the work and generate the plan, simulator and code.
  • Under our commercial terms with Anthropic, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models.
  • Generated output is a machine's work. It can be wrong, incomplete, or unsuitable for your purpose, and a generated plan is an estimate rather than a commitment. Where the product cannot do the real thing it labels the result as simulated instead of presenting it as real.
  • No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by automated means.

5. Shareable links

Plans, teasers, simulators and generated applications are published at long unguessable addresses so you can send them to a colleague or a client without either of you needing an account.

These links are unlisted, not secret. Anyone who has the address can open it, and we ask search engines not to index them, but a link forwarded onward is a link that works. Treat one like a document you emailed: share it deliberately, and ask us to revoke it if it gets loose.

6. The voice orb

You can describe your idea by talking instead of typing. Two different things can happen:

  • Dictation uses your browser's own speech recognition. On most browsers this runs on your device or through your browser vendor, under their privacy policy, not ours. We receive the resulting text, the same as if you had typed it.
  • Conversation, where enabled, connects you to a voice agent operated by ElevenLabs. Your audio and its transcript are processed by ElevenLabs to hold the conversation.

Narration audio elsewhere on the site is generated by Microsoft's Edge Read Aloud service, which receives only the text being spoken. Your microphone is never opened without you tapping the orb first, and your browser will ask for permission before it is.

9. Your rights

Email info@digit2ai.com saying which one you want. We will verify that you control the account, act within 30 days, and tell you if we need longer. We never charge for this, and using these rights will never degrade your service.

You can also delete individual projects from your workspace at any time, and unsubscribe from any email we send. If you are in the EEA or UK you may complain to your data protection authority; in California you may use an authorized agent, whose authority we will verify.

10. Security

  • Passwords are stored only as a salted bcrypt hash. We cannot read them.
  • Sessions are signed tokens with a 30-day expiry.
  • Password-reset links store only a one-way hash of the token, expire in one hour, and are destroyed on first use, so a stolen database yields no usable reset link.
  • Your workspace is scoped to your account identity, established by that signed session — not to a typed email address — so another person cannot cause a project to appear in your workspace or read one of yours.
  • Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS; the database is encrypted at rest by our host.
  • Uploaded files never touch disk, which removes a whole class of exposure.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required, without undue delay.
