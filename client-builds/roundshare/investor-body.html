02 — Company Overview

What RoundShare is

The product. A mobile-first AI riding assistant — upload a ride, get an AI ride score with a skill breakdown (rhythm, position, jump form, line & pace), strengths, areas to improve, and homework; then track progress and share it with friends, trainers, and barns. Live at roundshare.app

The company. RoundShare LLC — a Florida limited liability company owned in equal thirds by three founding members: a professional-equestrian CEO, a technology member (Digit2AI), and a business-development lead, plus a Founding Rider / youth ambassador.

The engine. Technology is delivered by Digit2AI LLC — an AI-native software company whose "RinglyPro Architect" build engine (an 83-agent workforce) compresses the cost and time of building and iterating the product. This is RoundShare's structural cost advantage versus a conventionally-staffed startup.

The ecosystem. RoundShare is the US community layer of a three-platform equestrian family — alongside Digit2AI's EquiMind (JumpCoach), Colombia and PasoFinoPro Global — giving shared AI infrastructure and multi-market reach.

Who pays. Competitive hunter/jumper, dressage, and eventing riders — and, critically, junior riders and their parents, a segment with proven willingness to pay for coaching, shows, and improvement. Trainers and barns are a natural B2B2C channel.

Why now. Model. Freemium consumer app → premium rider subscription (monthly), with trainer/barn tiers and a parent dashboard. Bilingual EN/ES from day one opens both the US and LATAM markets, reinforced by the sister EquiMind (Colombia) and PasoFinoPro (global) platforms.

05 — Valuation Methodology

Three independent lenses

A. Cost / Asset-Replacement Approach Floor

Values the built product by the cost to rebuild it conventionally: a mobile-first AI-analysis app (40-screen UX + scoring pipeline), an integrated voice AI, a full brand and icon system, a live bilingual site with database capture, and legal/e-signature infrastructure. Conventionally this represents an estimated 3–6 engineer-years; at a fully-loaded $150K–$200K/year that is $450K–$1.2M. Adding the owned brand/domain, the proprietary scoring design, and access to the Digit2AI build engine yields an asset floor of $0.75M–$1.5M.

B. Market-Comparable (Revenue Multiple) Primary at scale

Consumer / prosumer SaaS transacts on forward revenue multiples. Applying current-market ranges to scenario ARR:

RoundShare is one of three equestrian platforms in the EquiMind family (RoundShare US, EquiMind/JumpCoach Colombia, PasoFinoPro Global) sharing one AI engine. As the community/social layer, RoundShare compounds through network effects (riders invite trainers, barns, and friends), and the shared engine lowers the marginal cost of each new discipline, geography, and feature — raising the portfolio's expected value versus a single-market app.

08 — Risk Factors & Value Drivers

What moves the number

Risks / discounts

  • Pre-launch, pre-revenue — no traction, retention, or ARR yet; the single largest gate between the $2M and $10M ranges.
  • Production AI model unbuilt — the UX and pipeline exist; the real video-scoring model still needs to be trained and validated.
  • Niche category — equestrian is passionate but smaller than mainstream sports; TAM depends on penetration of competition riders + juniors.
  • Technology-partner dependency — engineering is delivered by Digit2AI (a member); Digit2AI's background IP is licensed to, not owned by, RoundShare.
  • Seasonality & hardware — riding/show seasonality and reliance on phone-captured video quality.

Value drivers / upside

  • Product already built — a demonstrable 40-screen app + live bilingual site, not a pitch deck.
  • Domain-expert CEO — a professional equestrian defines what "great riding" is — the labeling moat AI competitors lack.
  • AI-native cost advantage — Digit2AI's build engine compresses the cost/time of shipping and iterating.
  • Community flywheel — riders bring trainers, barns, and friends; sharing is the growth loop, not an afterthought.
  • Multi-market ecosystem — bilingual, with sister platforms in Colombia and globally.
  • Willing-to-pay buyer — junior riders + parents already spend heavily on the sport.

Why this range — not higher, not lower. With no users or revenue yet, a buyer has nothing to apply a growth multiple to, so the price is set by replacement-cost-plus (what it would cost them to reach this point, plus a premium for the head start) — not a revenue multiple. That premium above the build cost comes from four things: (1) a working, demonstrable product; (2) the roundshare.app brand and domain; (3) the founding team — especially the CEO's professional-equestrian expertise, the “what does great riding look like” labeling moat a buyer can't acquire elsewhere; and (4) strategic fit for the right acquirer.

Three things that swing the number.

  • Does the team transfer? With the founders (and the CEO's expertise) staying, it earns the top of the range or an acqui-hire premium. Code + brand only, team walking away, discounts toward the ~$1M floor.
  • DIGIT2AI technology is not included. Digit2AI's underlying AI engine (Background IP) is licensed to RoundShare, not owned by it, and per the Operating Agreement cannot be reverse-engineered or resold. A buyer gets the RoundShare product, brand, and Company IP plus a license to keep using the engine inside RoundShare — which caps what a pure-technology acquirer pays for “the software.”
  • All three members must agree. Carrie, Lala, and DIGIT2AI each own one-third; selling the company or material IP requires unanimous consent.
Recommendation. The single biggest, cheapest value unlock is to launch and get a few thousand engaged riders + a couple of barn/trainer partnerships before selling. That moves the story off replacement cost (~$2–3M) and onto traction ($6M–$10M+). Selling pre-launch hands the buyer that upside for the price of the parts. If you do sell now, prefer a strategic buyer in the equestrian world (show-circuit/federation org, equestrian media/brand, or a sports-video platform) over a financial buyer.
