02 — The Marketing Strategy

Barn-first: the trainer is the channel, not the obstacle
RoundShare is not a consumer app that hopes barns follow. It is a barn-first B2B2C platform: the trainer is the trusted entry point, riders are the users, and the consumer sharing loop is an amplifier — not the engine. One trainer onboards 8–15 riders at once, with built-in credibility no ad can buy.

The original plan bet everything on a consumer viral loop — the single highest-probability way the venture dies. The marketing strategy below makes barns the unit of growth, so no single channel failing is fatal: the largest channel has a floor, the riskiest channel has a cap and a kill switch, and the fragile channel (referral) is no longer load-bearing.

Failure mode it neutralizesHow barn-first marketing solves it
Referral loop never firesBarn rosters provide a floor of ~300–400 riders even if consumer sharing underperforms
AI not credible to expertsThe trainer endorses and verifies scores inside the app — the most dangerous critic becomes the distribution channel
Filming frictionBarns have fixed arenas: one mounted phone position, lesson-time filming routines, trainers who already film students

08 — Where the Angel Money Comes From

Raise inside the sport we're building for

Who to target ($350K, ~7–14 checks)

  • Barn & facility owners and successful trainers who already believe in the problem.
  • Junior-rider parents with means — the segment that spends heavily on the sport.
  • Show-circuit & federation figures (hunter/jumper, dressage, eventing) — strategic, well-networked.
  • Equestrian-adjacent operators — tack/feed/media businesses, breed associations.
  • Digit2AI's own network of prior angels and platform partners.

How we reach them

  • The 20-barn pilot is the pitch: a partnering barn is a warm introduction to the money in that barn.
  • Pony Finals / show-circuit presence doubles as an investor-sourcing venue.
  • The Gate 0 scoring number ("AI matches a pro's eye N% of the time") pre-empts the credibility question in every conversation.
  • Live demo + this deck — product built, not promised, de-risks the ask.
  • $25K minimum check keeps the round accessible to individual equestrian angels.
The strategic read. Roughly $30K of Gate-0 spend (scoring validation + $5K paid test + Pivo letter) decides whether the $350K angel close is worth pitching at all. Spend that $30K first, in that order — then raise the $350K from the sport, launch barn-first, and let the milestone gate unlock the $400K.
