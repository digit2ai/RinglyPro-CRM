ARTICLE I – FORMATION

1.1 Formation. The Company was formed as a Florida limited liability company upon the filing of its Articles of Organization with the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, pursuant to the Act. The Members ratify and approve such filing.

1.2 Name. The name of the Company is RoundShare LLC. The Company may conduct business under such fictitious names as approved and registered, including “RoundShare” and “RoundShare.app.”

1.3 Principal Office and Registered Agent. The principal office of the Company shall be located in the State of Florida at such address as the Members may designate. The Company shall continuously maintain a registered agent and registered office in Florida as required by the Act.

1.4 Term. The Company commenced on the date its Articles of Organization became effective and shall continue perpetually unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement and the Act.

1.5 Purpose. The Company is organized to develop, own, operate, market, and commercialize the RoundShare digital platform for equestrian athletes, including video organizing and sharing, ride scoring and feedback, educational content, and community features, and to engage in any lawful business permitted under the Act.

ARTICLE II – MISSION

The mission of the Company is to build the leading digital platform for equestrian athletes through technology, education, feedback, and community. New During the Temporary Ownership Period, the RoundShare digital platform — including the RoundShare.app landing experience, the interactive in-browser application simulator, the AI ride-analysis and scoring engine, the “Lina” voice-AI narration, the waitlist and data-capture systems, brand assets and marks (including “RoundShare” and the roundshare.app domain), source code, designs, and related work product (collectively, the “RoundShare Platform”) — is owned solely and exclusively by DIGIT2AI. Any work product created by any party, contractor, or the Founding Rider for the RoundShare Platform during this period is a contribution to, and owned by, DIGIT2AI, and each such person hereby assigns such work product to DIGIT2AI.

6.1B Contribution to the Company Upon Vesting. New Effective upon Vesting, DIGIT2AI contributes, assigns, and transfers to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to the RoundShare Platform (excluding DIGIT2AI Background IP under §6.3, which is licensed under §6.4), whereupon the RoundShare Platform becomes “Company IP.” If Vesting does not occur, the RoundShare Platform remains the sole property of DIGIT2AI.

6.1 Company IP. From and after Vesting, all software, source code, AI prompts and agent configurations, models and training artifacts created specifically for the Company, designs, user interfaces, websites, logos, trademarks (including “RoundShare” and the roundshare.app domain), documentation, product specifications, databases, user data, and related work product created by or for the Company (collectively, “Company IP”) are and shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

6.2 Assignment. From and after Vesting, each Member irrevocably assigns, and shall cause its personnel, representatives, and contractors to assign, to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to Company IP, including all copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and moral rights, worldwide, and shall execute such further documents as the Company reasonably requests to perfect such ownership.

6.3 Background IP of DIGIT2AI — Sole and Exclusive Ownership. Revised

  • (a) Definition and ownership. The parties acknowledge and irrevocably agree that DIGIT2AI owns, solely and exclusively, all pre-existing and independently developed technology, including its enterprise-architecture methodology, agent-orchestration frameworks, MCP server infrastructure, shared voice/text-to-speech and neural-intelligence services, machine-learning models and pipelines, reusable software components, libraries, templates, tools, algorithms, prompts, and know-how developed outside of or prior to work for the Company, together with all improvements, modifications, and derivatives of the foregoing of general applicability (collectively, “DIGIT2AI Background IP”). DIGIT2AI Background IP is not Company IP, is not contributed to the Company, and is and shall at all times remain the sole and exclusive property of DIGIT2AI. No provision of this Agreement, and nothing about the operation of the RoundShare Platform, transfers, assigns, or grants to the Company, to Carrie Dickenson, to Maria Clara Garcia, or to any other person any ownership interest in DIGIT2AI Background IP. The Company’s only rights in DIGIT2AI Background IP are the limited license granted in §6.4, which arises solely upon Vesting.
  • (b) No reverse engineering. Neither the Company nor Carrie Dickenson, Maria Clara Garcia, nor any of their respective personnel, agents, contractors, successors, or affiliates shall, directly or indirectly, reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, decrypt, extract, re-create, or otherwise attempt to derive, discover, or reconstruct the source code, model weights, architecture, methods, or trade secrets of any DIGIT2AI Background IP, nor authorize or assist any third party to do so, by any means and regardless of whether the RoundShare Platform embeds or exposes such technology.
  • (c) No sale or transfer. Neither the Company nor Carrie Dickenson nor Maria Clara Garcia shall sell, sublicense, lease, lend, distribute, publish, disclose, pledge, encumber, assign, or otherwise transfer or commercialize DIGIT2AI Background IP, in whole or in part, or any derivative of it, to any person, whether or not for value. Any purported sale or transfer in violation of this Section is void.
  • (d) No competing or derivative use. Neither Carrie Dickenson nor Maria Clara Garcia shall use DIGIT2AI Background IP, or any knowledge of it gained through the Company, for any purpose other than operating the RoundShare Platform, and in particular shall not use it to build, train, or improve any product for themselves or any third party.
  • (e) Enforcement. The parties agree that DIGIT2AI Background IP includes DIGIT2AI trade secrets, that a breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and that DIGIT2AI is entitled to immediate injunctive relief (without the mediation-first requirement of §13.2 and without posting bond) in addition to all other remedies. The obligations of this Section are perpetual, bind the Founding Officers whether or not they become Members, and survive the lapse of any earn-in right, any Member’s withdrawal, and the dissolution of the Company.

6.4 License to the Company. Effective upon Vesting, DIGIT2AI grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use DIGIT2AI Background IP solely as embedded in or necessary to operate, maintain, and further develop the RoundShare Platform. This license survives DIGIT2AI’s withdrawal or the transfer of its Membership Interest, and survives dissolution as to any successor that acquires the RoundShare Platform. Prior to Vesting, DIGIT2AI operates the RoundShare Platform as its own property and no such license is required.

6.5 Non-Compete Scope — Limited to RoundShare Only. Revised

  • (a) The only restriction. The sole non-competition restriction under this Agreement is this: while a Member (or, as to a Founding Officer, from the Effective Date and while holding an earn-in right or Membership Interest) and for eighteen (18) months thereafter, no Member and neither Founding Officer shall develop, own, operate, or commercialize a product that directly replicates the RoundShare Platform — that is, a video-recording, ride-scoring, feedback, and social-sharing platform targeted at the equestrian market. Nothing else in this Agreement restricts any party’s other business activities.
  • (b) DIGIT2AI expansion expressly permitted. DIGIT2AI is a technology company that builds and operates many products, verticals, and platforms. Except for building a direct clone of the RoundShare Platform as described in (a), nothing in this Agreement limits DIGIT2AI’s development, ownership, operation, expansion, partnership, or commercialization of any other product, vertical, or technology, including in the equestrian and animal-athlete space and adjacent categories (for example, gait analysis, conformation, training, breed-specific tools, and coaching products). Such activities are expressly deemed not competitive with the Company.
  • (c) Existing partnerships acknowledged. Without limiting (b), the parties acknowledge that DIGIT2AI is currently a partner in, and independently develops and operates, ventures including JumpCoach and PasoFino Pro. These and DIGIT2AI’s other current and future partnerships, products, and verticals are permitted, are not restricted by this Agreement, and are not competitive with the Company, provided that Company IP and Confidential Information are not used in violation of this Agreement (DIGIT2AI Background IP under §6.3 remains DIGIT2AI’s to use freely).
  • (d) Founding Officers. This §6.5 binds Carrie Dickenson and Maria Clara Garcia from the Effective Date, whether or not they have become Members.

6.6 Third-Party Materials. Open-source software shall be used in compliance with applicable licenses, and no copyleft license shall be applied to Company IP in a manner that requires public disclosure of proprietary Company source code without, after Vesting, majority approval of the Members.

ARTICLE IX – TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS AND MEMBER DEPARTURE

9.1 Restriction on Transfer. No Member may sell, assign, pledge, or otherwise transfer all or any portion of its Membership Interest, and no Founding Officer may transfer an earn-in right, without complying with this Article. Any purported transfer in violation of this Article is void. A change of control of DIGIT2AI (meaning Manuel Stagg ceasing to control DIGIT2AI) shall be treated as a proposed transfer subject to this Article.

9.2 Right of First Opportunity. If a Member desires to withdraw or transfer its Membership Interest (a “Departing Member”), the remaining Members shall have the right of first opportunity, exercisable within sixty (60) days of written notice, to purchase the Departing Member’s Membership Interest pro rata (or in such other proportions as the remaining Members agree).

9.3 Valuation. The purchase price shall be the fair market value of the Membership Interest as mutually agreed by the Departing Member and the remaining Members. If the parties cannot agree within thirty (30) days, fair market value shall be determined by an independent valuation professional jointly selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding; costs shall be shared equally between the Departing Member and the purchasing Members.

9.4 Payment Terms. Unless otherwise agreed, the purchase price may be paid over up to twenty-four (24) months in equal monthly installments with simple interest at the applicable federal rate, evidenced by a promissory note.

9.5 Effect of Departure. Upon closing, the Departing Member ceases to be a Member and retains no rights other than the right to payment. Confidentiality and IP-assignment obligations survive. If DIGIT2AI is the Departing Member, the license in §6.4 survives in full.
