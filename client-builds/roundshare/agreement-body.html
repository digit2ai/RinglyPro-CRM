ROUNDSHARE LLC

A Florida Limited Liability Company
Effective Date: __________________  ·  Version 4.0

This Operating Agreement (this “Agreement”) of RoundShare LLC, a Florida limited liability company (the “Company”), is entered into as of the Effective Date set forth on the signature page by and among the persons and entities identified in Article III as Members (each a “Member” and collectively the “Members”), pursuant to the Florida Revised Limited Liability Company Act, Chapter 605, Florida Statutes, as amended (the “Act”).

RoundShare was founded as an equal partnership among Carrie Dickenson, Visionarium LLC, and DIGIT2AI LLC. For the avoidance of doubt, Manuel Stagg, in his individual capacity, is not a Member of the Company and holds no membership interest; DIGIT2AI LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“DIGIT2AI”), is the Member, and Manuel Stagg acts solely as the designated representative of DIGIT2AI. Likewise, Maria Clara Garcia, in her individual capacity, is not a Member of the Company and holds no membership interest; Visionarium LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“Visionarium”), is the Member, and Maria Clara Garcia acts solely as the designated representative of Visionarium.

ARTICLE I – FORMATION

1.1 Formation. The Company was formed as a Florida limited liability company upon the filing of its Articles of Organization with the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, pursuant to the Act. The Members ratify and approve such filing.

1.2 Name. The name of the Company is RoundShare LLC. The Company may conduct business under such fictitious names as the Members approve and register, including “RoundShare” and “RoundShare.app.”

1.3 Principal Office and Registered Agent. The principal office of the Company shall be located in the State of Florida at such address as the Members may designate. The Company shall continuously maintain a registered agent and registered office in Florida as required by the Act.

1.4 Term. The Company commenced on the date its Articles of Organization became effective and shall continue perpetually unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement and the Act.

1.5 Purpose. The Company is organized to develop, own, operate, market, and commercialize the RoundShare digital platform for equestrian athletes, including video organizing and sharing, ride scoring and feedback, educational content, and community features, and to engage in any lawful business permitted under the Act.

ARTICLE II – MISSION

The mission of the Company is to build the leading digital platform for equestrian athletes through technology, education, feedback, and community. The Founding Principles attached as Exhibit A express the shared values of the Members but do not create independently enforceable obligations except as expressly stated elsewhere in this Agreement.

ARTICLE VI – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Company IP. All software, source code, AI prompts and agent configurations, models and training artifacts created specifically for the Company, designs, user interfaces, websites, logos, trademarks (including “RoundShare” and the roundshare.app domain), documentation, product specifications, databases, user data, and related work product created by or for the Company, or created by any Member, representative, contractor, or the Founding Rider specifically for the Company (collectively, “Company IP”), are and shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

6.2 Assignment. Each Member hereby irrevocably assigns, and shall cause its personnel, representatives, and contractors to assign, to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to Company IP, including all copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and moral rights, worldwide. Each Member shall execute such further documents as the Company reasonably requests to perfect such ownership.

6.3 Background IP of DIGIT2AI — Sole and Exclusive Ownership.

  • (a) Definition and ownership. The Members acknowledge and irrevocably agree that DIGIT2AI owns, solely and exclusively, all pre-existing and independently developed technology, including its enterprise-architecture methodology, agent-orchestration frameworks, MCP server infrastructure, shared voice/text-to-speech and neural-intelligence services, machine-learning models and pipelines, reusable software components, libraries, templates, tools, algorithms, prompts, and know-how developed outside of or prior to work for the Company, together with all improvements, modifications, and derivatives of the foregoing of general applicability (collectively, “DIGIT2AI Background IP”). DIGIT2AI Background IP is not Company IP and is and shall at all times remain the sole and exclusive property of DIGIT2AI. No provision of this Agreement, and nothing about the operation of the RoundShare platform, transfers, assigns, or grants to the Company, to Carrie Dickenson, to Visionarium, or to any other person any ownership interest in DIGIT2AI Background IP. The Company’s only rights in DIGIT2AI Background IP are the limited license granted in §6.4.
  • (b) No reverse engineering. Neither the Company nor Carrie Dickenson, Visionarium, nor any of their respective personnel, agents, contractors, successors, or affiliates shall, directly or indirectly, reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, decrypt, extract, re-create, or otherwise attempt to derive, discover, or reconstruct the source code, model weights, architecture, methods, or trade secrets of any DIGIT2AI Background IP, nor authorize or assist any third party to do so, by any means and regardless of whether the RoundShare platform embeds or exposes such technology.
  • (c) No sale or transfer. Neither the Company nor Carrie Dickenson nor Visionarium shall sell, sublicense, lease, lend, distribute, publish, disclose, pledge, encumber, assign, or otherwise transfer or commercialize DIGIT2AI Background IP, in whole or in part, or any derivative of it, to any person, whether or not for value. Any purported sale or transfer in violation of this Section is void.
  • (d) No competing or derivative use. Neither Carrie Dickenson nor Visionarium shall use DIGIT2AI Background IP, or any knowledge of it gained through the Company, for any purpose other than operating the RoundShare platform, and in particular shall not use it to build, train, or improve any product for themselves or any third party.
  • (e) Enforcement. The parties agree that DIGIT2AI Background IP includes DIGIT2AI trade secrets, that a breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and that DIGIT2AI is entitled to immediate injunctive relief (without the mediation-first requirement of §13.2 and without posting bond) in addition to all other remedies. The obligations of this Section are perpetual and survive any Member’s withdrawal and the dissolution of the Company.

6.4 License to the Company. DIGIT2AI grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to use DIGIT2AI Background IP solely as embedded in or necessary to operate, maintain, and further develop the RoundShare platform. During the period that DIGIT2AI is a Member and provides ongoing platform, infrastructure, and maintenance services to the Company, the Company’s exercise of this license is subject to the DIGIT2AI Technology License & Services Fee set forth in Schedule B. Upon DIGIT2AI’s withdrawal, the transfer of its Membership Interest, or the dissolution of the Company, this license continues on a royalty-free basis as to the Company and any successor that acquires the RoundShare platform, and no further fee is payable. This license survives DIGIT2AI’s withdrawal or the transfer of its Membership Interest, and survives dissolution as to any successor that acquires the RoundShare platform. The license does not permit any use of DIGIT2AI Background IP outside the RoundShare platform, nor any act prohibited by §6.3.

6.5 Non-Compete Scope — Limited to the RoundShare Product Only.

  • (a) The only restriction. The sole non-competition restriction under this Agreement is narrow: while a Member and for eighteen (18) months thereafter, no Member shall use the Company’s Company IP or Confidential Information to develop, own, operate, or commercialize a product that copies or directly replicates the Company’s specific RoundShare product, brand, or codebase. A product is not restricted merely because it operates in the same general category as RoundShare (i.e., a video-recording, ride-scoring, feedback, or social-sharing platform for the equestrian market); only the copying or misappropriation of the Company’s own RoundShare product, brand, and Company IP is prohibited. Nothing else in this Agreement restricts any Member’s other business activities.
  • (b) DIGIT2AI expansion expressly permitted. DIGIT2AI is a technology company that builds and operates many products, verticals, and platforms. Except for copying the Company’s specific RoundShare product as described in (a), nothing in this Agreement limits DIGIT2AI’s development, ownership, operation, expansion, partnership, or commercialization of any other product, vertical, or technology, including other video-recording, ride-scoring, feedback, and social-sharing platforms for the equestrian market and adjacent categories (for example, gait analysis, conformation, training, breed-specific tools, and coaching products). Such activities are expressly deemed not competitive with the Company.
  • (c) DIGIT2AI’s existing equestrian platforms are permitted and non-competitive. The Members expressly acknowledge and agree that DIGIT2AI already owns, operates, and independently develops two other video-recording, ride-scoring, feedback, and social-sharing platforms for the equestrian market — EquiMind (JumpCoach), Colombia, and PasoFinoPro Global — which predate and/or are independent of the Company. These platforms, and DIGIT2AI’s continued operation, development, expansion, and commercialization of them and of any other current or future DIGIT2AI venture, are expressly permitted, are not restricted by this Agreement, and are not competitive with the Company, notwithstanding that they operate in the same general category as RoundShare, provided that the Company’s Company IP and Confidential Information are not used in violation of this Agreement. DIGIT2AI Background IP under §6.3 remains DIGIT2AI’s to use freely across all of its platforms.
  • (d) Equal application. This §6.5 applies equally to all Members.

6.6 Third-Party Materials. Open-source software shall be used in compliance with applicable licenses, and no copyleft license shall be applied to Company IP in a manner that requires public disclosure of proprietary Company source code without majority approval of the Members.

ARTICLE VII – CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Obligation. Each Member shall hold in strict confidence, and shall not use except for the benefit of the Company or disclose except as required by law, all non-public information of the Company, including business plans, financial information, product roadmaps, source code, user data, and partner terms (“Confidential Information”). Each Member is responsible for compliance by its personnel and representatives, including, in the case of DIGIT2AI, the DIGIT2AI Representative.

7.2 Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Member, was lawfully known prior to disclosure, or is independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

7.3 User Data and Minors. The Company serves junior riders, many of whom are minors. The Members shall cause the Company to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable state privacy statutes, and to maintain a published privacy policy governing collection and use of video, biometric-adjacent, and personal data.

7.4 Survival. The obligations of this Article survive a Member’s withdrawal and the dissolution of the Company for a period of five (5) years, and indefinitely as to trade secrets.

ARTICLE VIII – VOTING

8.1 Voting Power. Each Member votes in proportion to its Membership Interest. DIGIT2AI votes through the DIGIT2AI Representative.

8.2 Ordinary Matters. Except as provided in §8.3, all matters require the approval of Members holding a majority of the Membership Interests.

8.3 Unanimous Matters. The following actions require the unanimous written approval of all Members:

  • Selling, merging, or otherwise disposing of the Company or all or substantially all of its assets
  • Changing the Membership Interests or admitting new Members
  • Raising investment capital or incurring indebtedness in excess of $10,000
  • Amending this Agreement or the Articles of Organization
  • Dissolving the Company
  • Selling, exclusively licensing, or encumbering material Company IP
  • Entering into any transaction between the Company and a Member or an affiliate of a Member, other than the services and licenses expressly described in this Agreement

8.4 Action Without Meeting. Any action may be taken without a meeting by written consent (including electronic signature or email confirmation) signed by Members holding the required voting threshold.

8.5 Deadlock. If the Members are deadlocked on an ordinary matter for more than thirty (30) days, any Member may refer the matter to mediation under §13.2 before pursuing any other remedy.

ARTICLE IX – TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS AND MEMBER DEPARTURE

9.1 Restriction on Transfer. No Member may sell, assign, pledge, or otherwise transfer all or any portion of its Membership Interest without complying with this Article. Any purported transfer in violation of this Article is void. A change of control of DIGIT2AI (meaning Manuel Stagg ceasing to control DIGIT2AI) shall be treated as a proposed transfer subject to this Article.

9.2 Right of First Opportunity. If a Member desires to withdraw or transfer its Membership Interest (a “Departing Member”), the remaining Members shall have the right of first opportunity, exercisable within sixty (60) days of written notice, to purchase the Departing Member’s Membership Interest pro rata (or in such other proportions as the remaining Members agree).

9.3 Valuation. The purchase price shall be the fair market value of the Membership Interest as mutually agreed by the Departing Member and the remaining Members. If the parties cannot agree within thirty (30) days, fair market value shall be determined by an independent valuation professional jointly selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding; costs shall be shared equally between the Departing Member and the purchasing Members.

9.4 Payment Terms. Unless otherwise agreed, the purchase price may be paid over up to twenty-four (24) months in equal monthly installments with simple interest at the applicable federal rate, evidenced by a promissory note.

9.5 Effect of Departure. Upon closing, the Departing Member ceases to be a Member and retains no rights other than the right to payment. Confidentiality and IP-assignment obligations survive. If DIGIT2AI is the Departing Member, the license in §6.4 and the ownership and protections of §6.3 survive in full.

ARTICLE X – FINANCES, ALLOCATIONS, DISTRIBUTIONS, AND RECORDS

10.1 Separate Funds. Company funds shall be maintained in one or more accounts in the Company’s name and shall not be commingled with the personal or business funds of any Member.

10.2 Allocations. Profits and losses shall be allocated among the Members in proportion to their Membership Interests, subject to any regulatory allocations required under Section 704 of the Internal Revenue Code and the Treasury Regulations.

10.3 Distributions. Distributions shall be made at such times and in such amounts as the Members determine by majority vote, in proportion to Membership Interests. The Members shall use commercially reasonable efforts to distribute, no later than April 1 of each year, an amount sufficient to cover each Member’s estimated tax liability attributable to Company income allocated for the prior year, to the extent of available cash.

ARTICLE XI – INDEMNIFICATION

To the fullest extent permitted by the Act, the Company shall indemnify and hold harmless each Member, the DIGIT2AI Representative, and each officer from and against losses, claims, and expenses arising out of actions taken in good faith on behalf of the Company, except to the extent arising from fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, or a knowing violation of law. ARTICLE XII – DISSOLUTION

12.1 Events of Dissolution. The Company shall dissolve only upon (a) the unanimous written consent of the Members, or (b) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act.

12.2 Winding Up. Upon dissolution, the Company’s affairs shall be wound up, assets liquidated, and proceeds applied first to creditors (including Members that are creditors), then to the establishment of reasonable reserves, and finally to the Members in proportion to their positive capital-account balances and thereafter in proportion to Membership Interests.

12.3 IP on Dissolution. DIGIT2AI Background IP is not an asset of the Company and is not subject to winding up; it remains the sole property of DIGIT2AI. Company IP may be sold or licensed in winding up only with unanimous consent, and any acquirer of the RoundShare platform receives the benefit of the license in §6.4, subject to §6.3.

13.5 Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement remains in effect and the unenforceable provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

13.6 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, and electronic signatures (including through this platform, DocuSign, or equivalent) are as effective as original signatures.

13.7 Notices. Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized courier, or email with confirmation of receipt to the addresses on file in the Company’s records.

SCHEDULE B – DIGIT2AI TECHNOLOGY LICENSE & SERVICES FEE

B.1 Purpose and related-party acknowledgment. This Schedule sets the fee payable by the Company to DIGIT2AI for the ongoing license of DIGIT2AI Background IP under §6.4 and the shared technology and infrastructure services DIGIT2AI provides to operate the RoundShare platform. The Members acknowledge that DIGIT2AI is both a Member of the Company (33⅓%) and the licensor and service provider under this Schedule, and that this is therefore an affiliated, related-party arrangement. The Members, by unanimous consent under Article VIII, approve this Schedule and agree that the fee set forth below is intended to be fair to the Company and at or below the amount an unaffiliated licensor would charge for equivalent rights and services.

B.2 Services covered. For the fee below, DIGIT2AI provides, on a continuing basis: (a) the license to use DIGIT2AI Background IP under §6.4; (b) access to and operation of the shared neural-intelligence, voice/text-to-speech, and MCP-orchestration infrastructure used by the platform; (c) hosting, DevOps, security, and platform maintenance; and (d) ongoing engineering, model updates, and technical support as reasonably required to keep the platform in service.

B.3 Fee — cost recovery only (no royalty). Because DIGIT2AI is a Member of the Company and already shares in the Company’s profits through its Membership Interest, DIGIT2AI shall not charge the Company any revenue royalty or profit-based fee. The monthly fee is limited to cost recovery and is computed as:

Monthly Fee = (2 × Fixed Platform-Maintenance Cost) + AI API Token Cost

  • (a) Fixed Platform-Maintenance Cost (charged at 2×). DIGIT2AI’s documented monthly cost of hosting, database, infrastructure, monitoring, security, and maintenance attributable to the RoundShare platform, excluding AI/LLM usage costs. This component is charged at twice (2×) such documented cost (cost plus 100%) to cover DIGIT2AI’s engineering and support overhead. The Members estimate this component at approximately US $400 per month (i.e., ~$200 documented cost × 2) at the date of this Agreement.
  • (b) AI API Token Cost (charged at cost, pass-through). DIGIT2AI’s actual documented cost of the third-party AI / LLM API tokens consumed by users’ use of the platform’s AI analysis and voice features during the month, charged at cost with no markup. This component scales directly up and down with subscribers’ consumption of AI, so the fee rises only as usage rises.

If the Company lacks sufficient cash, the Members may, by majority vote of the disinterested Members, elect to accrue (defer) all or part of the fixed component (a) until the Company has adequate funds; accrued amounts bear no interest. The pass-through AI API token cost (b) is reimbursed to DIGIT2AI as incurred.

B.4 Payment, statements, and audit. The fee is payable monthly in arrears within fifteen (15) days after each month-end. DIGIT2AI shall deliver a monthly statement itemizing the documented fixed maintenance cost (and its 2× amount), the actual AI API token cost, and the total amount due. The Company may, at its expense and on reasonable notice, audit DIGIT2AI’s supporting records for the fee not more than once per calendar year.

B.5 Operating expense; not a distribution. The fee is an ordinary operating expense of the Company paid before any distribution to Members under Article X. It is compensation for license rights and services, is not a distribution, and does not change any Member’s Membership Interest.

B.6 Fairness safeguards and amendment. Because DIGIT2AI is an interested Member, DIGIT2AI shall abstain from any vote to increase the fee under this Schedule, and any increase or other amendment to this Schedule requires the prior written consent of the disinterested Members (Carrie Dickenson and Visionarium LLC). The Members shall review this Schedule at least annually and adjust it only to keep the fee at or below fair market value.

B.7 Term and survival. The fee is payable only while DIGIT2AI is a Member and provides the services described in B.2. Consistent with §6.4, upon DIGIT2AI’s withdrawal, the transfer of its Membership Interest, or the dissolution of the Company, the license continues royalty-free as to the Company and any successor that acquires the RoundShare platform, and no further fee under this Schedule is payable.
